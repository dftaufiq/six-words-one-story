A complete story in exactly six words, written for make.fun's "Six Words, One Story" bounty.

> He came home. Nobody remembered him.

A man returns after a long absence to a home that no longer knows him. The turn lands in the last three words; the reader fills in where he was, how long he was gone, and why nobody remembers him. Six words, 36 bytes.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the story to `/output/entry.txt` (the whole entry — the six words are all there is).
